This document is a watchlist of papers and trends not yet folded into the canon of the tracked services (X, Meta, Netflix). Entries are chosen by relevance to production and written as 2–4 line pointers. Their lifespan is about 3 months, after which they are promoted or graduated. The detailed rules are in CLAUDE.md.

A daily batch handles collection. New arXiv cs.IR submissions are scanned and selected every day, and industry blogs (Google Research, Pinterest, Spotify, LinkedIn, etc.) and conference accepted lists are checked 1 time a week.

Watching (open threads)

Spread of LLM-native ranking — who follows after GenRec

  • What: A trend of replacing one role in the recommendation pipeline with an LLM. As of 2026-08, five first-party statements have reached production deployment, and their mechanisms differ verified. → llm-in-recsys.md
    • Details: The five and their mechanisms are as follows verified.
      • Netflix GenRec: scoring function
      • Yandex Gryphon-v2/Sona: unifying generative retrieval and rank
      • Alibaba MetaStrategy: offline policy compiler
      • Meta ConnectionMind: graph path reasoner
      • Meta AMBER: input tokenization
    • Details: Of these, only AMBER did not disclose online A/B figures, and the other four stated A/B figures.
    • Details: When the thread was opened, only the scoring function in the serving path was considered. Statements that came in afterward touched different roles, which widened the scope. When a new statement arrives it is added to this list, and the body does not count the order.
  • Why notable: Whether X (which uses an LLM for annotation) and Meta (which uses an LLM as a feature encoder) would take the same road was the biggest fork of the next 1 year. However, Meta entered with ConnectionMind in 2026-08 (⚠ the earlier proposition was half overturned).
  • Watch for: We await first-party statements that other companies (Google/YouTube, TikTok) deployed an LLM in the serving path, follow-up news on the full GenRec rollout, and follow-up posts on ConnectionMind's surface confirmation and serving cost.
  • 2026-08-12 additional evidence: Taobao MetaStrategy (arXiv 2608.09440, 2026-08-10) is a third angle, using the LLM as an offline policy compiler rather than placing it in the serving path. In a 7-day A/B on the "Guess You Like" surface it gained click PV +2.11%, IPV +3.12%, and GMV +2.83% verified.
    • Details: An LLM generates typed JSON (objective weights, category preferences, position policy, guardrails) nearline. A validator and compiler use it to instantiate a deterministic Generator, which competes atomically with existing strategies inside the GE (Generator-Evaluator) architecture.
    • Details: Because LLM inference is outside sync ranking, the RT impact is 0; in other words, there is no latency increase on the synchronous path.
    • Details: Unlike GenRec (scoring function) or Gryphon-v2 (generative retrieval and rank), this mechanism keeps the LLM outside the synchronous ranking path.
  • 2026-08-13 additional evidence (canon update, not radar): In Meta ConnectionMind (arXiv 2608.10187, 2026-08-10), Llama 3.1-8B is used as a path reasoner over a graph. Netflix GenRec is a "scoring function" and ConnectionMind is a "graph path reasoner", so a new point to watch arose: the substance of role 3 (using an LLM as a ranker) differs by company. → services/facebook.md, topics/llm-in-recsys.md
    • Details: This LLM serves the online hot path for heavy users, who make up 5–10% of users, and the other 90–95% are handled by a Student GNN trained on meta-paths that the LLM discovered offline.
    • Details: The paper does not disclose the surface and writes "short-form video platform", but the graph schema suggests it is likely Facebook. Watch time rose +0.43%.
  • 2026-08-28 additional evidence: Meta AMBER (arXiv 2608.25546, 2026-08-26) is an input tokenization mechanism that compresses each interaction event into one Event Token and feeds it into the LLM input. It serves billions of events per day on the Facebook surface with online encoding verified.
    • Details: It was written by 15 authors affiliated with "AI at Meta". Under the angle of Event Tokenization, it compresses the entire temporal snapshot of user, item, context, and outcome signals at each event into one compact Event Token and introduces it as a new token type in the LLM input.
    • Details: It improved the compute-quality Pareto frontier on industry-scale ranking and retrieval benchmarks, and it can be ported as a feature to non-LLM rankers (statistically significant). Online A/B figures are not disclosed in the abstract.
    • Details: It touches a different role from GenRec (scoring), ConnectionMind (graph reasoning), and MetaStrategy (offline policy compiler). That is, even within Meta, LLM-based recsys approaches have split into at least two axes (graph reasoner and input encoder).
  • 2026-09-07 additional evidence: Spotify GLIDE and LinkedIn Follows both count as placing fine-tuned LLM embeddings in a candidate generation channel. With these two sources the thread grows to six companies and seven statements (Netflix, Yandex, Alibaba, Meta (2), Spotify, LinkedIn). Detailed analysis: daily/2026-09-07.md
    • Details: Spotify GLIDE (From Models to Products: LLMs for Recommendation at Spotify Scale, 2026-08-03 blog) is a candidate generator based on a 1B LLM and Semantic ID. Semantic ID (SID) is an identifier that represents an item as a sequence of semantic tokens. GLIDE was deployed on the Spotify Home podcast discovery surface and supplies candidates to the downstream ranking pipeline.
    • Details: In a 21-day A/B at ~20M impression scale, GLIDE gained non-habitual podcast listening +5.4% and new-show discovery +14.3%, while keeping latency and engagement requirements verified.
    • Details: LinkedIn Follows is sourced from Rebuilding LinkedIn's Follows Recommendations with LLM-Based Semantic Retrieval and Ranking (2026-08-24 blog). A fine-tuned bi-encoder LLM rewrites profiles as natural-language sentences (narrative prompting) and then embeds them. Creator embeddings are built in a nightly FAISS batch, and viewer embeddings are inferred in real time with Proxima at onboarding.
    • Details: Follows was deployed on two surfaces, the MyNetwork tab and Home Feed, and in the production A/B the follow rate rose statistically significantly, with the largest lift in the new member segment verified. The specific uplift percentage is not disclosed.
    • Details: The two sources complement Pinterest CLR (an approach that conditions the user tower of the Home Feed candidate generator on retrieval context). Pinterest approaches by conditioning, LinkedIn by verbalizing profiles, and Spotify by SID generation.
  • 2026-10-12 additional evidence: In Pinterest User Journeys (From Activity to Intent, 2026-10-09 blog), an LLM generates user-level natural-language queries, and it is on the same side as MetaStrategy in that it is outside the synchronous path. Compared with clustering, the A/B showed email CTR +1.1% and push open +1.3% verified. Detailed analysis: daily/2026-10-12.md
    • Details: Qwen3-4B, distilled from a frontier teacher, reads 360-day activity logs and generates lists of journey names in batch. Each name becomes an embedding-based retrieval query on the notification surface.
  • 2026-10-19 additional evidence: The Spotify and NVIDIA TensorRT LLM collaboration (Advancing Generative Recommendation Inference, 2026-10-16 blog) covers the serving cost of podcast LLM recommendation, which has been in production since 2026-01. It writes that, on Qwen3 1.7B, beam search cost fell by up to 54% and scoring cost by up to 7% verified (the comparison baseline is not clear in the text). Detailed analysis: daily/2026-10-19.md
    • Details: It separates a generation workload, which uses beam search to pull top Semantic ID sequences and convert them to catalog items, from a workload that scores multiple candidates in one request with candidate masking verified.
    • Details: It personalizes by projecting an existing user embedding into a single soft token verified.
    • Details: This statement treats an LLM in the serving path not as "does it work" but as "at what cost does it work."
  • 2026-10-24 additional evidence: Kuaishou OneLatent (arXiv 2610.30675, 2026-10-22) compresses the explicit reasoning sentences of an LLM-based recommendation model into a few trainable latent tokens and answers without generating reasoning sentences. The abstract reports an estimated revenue of +9.6% over OneRec and OneReason in an online A/B test on a local-services ads surface verified. Details: daily/2026-10-24.md
    • Details: The abstract says SID@64 is 17.44% higher than the Think variant, which uses explicit reasoning, and that online inference throughput is at least 17× higher verified. Because the surface is limited to local-services ads, it is unknown whether this carries over to feed surfaces inferred.

Recommendation model scaling laws — independent confirmations from companies are accumulating

  • What: Cross-checks the claim that recommendation models too "improve predictably like LLMs as data and parameters grow". The evidence so far comes in three strands: → sequence-transformer-ranking.md
    • Meta HSTU (a sequence model in the organic feed lineage) and Wukong verified
    • Netflix FM (a Foundation Model that builds member representations centrally) with its "consistent improvements" and the 2M→1B shadow study verified
    • Meta ads LLaTTE ("semantic features are a prerequisite") verified
  • Why notable: If scaling holds, recommendation also becomes an arms race of "bigger models + longer sequences". However, the LLaTTE and HSTU claims are opposite on whether semantic features are a prerequisite, and this issue is not yet resolved.
  • Watch for: We await counterexamples and cases that disclose parameter counts.
    • Details: Statements outside Meta and Netflix, in order of arXiv submission date, are ByteDance TM20K (2026-08-07), Ant Densing Law (2026-08-24), and Alibaba TransRetrieval (2026-08-26).
    • Details: After that came ByteDance ReST (2026-09-01) and HELIX (2026-09-29, [inferred] TikTok).
  • 2026-08-28 additional evidence: Alibaba/Alimama TransRetrieval (arXiv 2608.25528, 2026-08-26) is the company that stated a scaling law after Meta HSTU, Wukong, and LLaTTE, and the first statement on the retrieval side. A one-month production A/B on the Alibaba display ads platform gave revenue +2.53% (95% CI [2.22, 2.70], p<0.0001) and RPM +1.28% verified.
    • Details: It was written jointly by 6 authors from Taobao & Tmall Group and 4 from RUC. It is an ads retrieval framework that suppresses token-norm divergence caused by field heterogeneity with weighted average aggregation, so that the Transformer scales.
    • Details: Target token compression cut compute per candidate by 85%, and position-style domain embedding unified multiple domains.
    • Details: The same A/B kept latency unchanged (P99 ~40ms), and split into 4 domains the gains were A +1.72% / B +2.51% / C +5.39% / D +0.87% verified.
    • Details: LLaTTE holds that "semantic features are needed" and HSTU holds that "scaling alone suffices". A third answer is added: "scaling is possible if field heterogeneity is handled explicitly."
  • 2026-09-09 additional evidence (late discovery): Shopify Foundational Generative Recommender (Shopify Engineering, 2026-02-25) deployed to production for product recommendations an autoregressive sequence Transformer, based on HSTU and adapted to e-commerce. It is the first statement from the e-commerce domain confirming that the HSTU license is accessible enough for other companies to adopt in production. Detailed analysis: daily/2026-09-09.md
    • Details: The deployment surfaces are product recommendations in the Shop app and merchant storefronts.
    • Details: However, it does not disclose the identity or parameter scale of the baseline model, so the strength it adds to this thread is weak.
    • Details: It uses raw buyer event sequences (search, view, cart, purchase) directly as input. It combines RoPE-based rotary encoding, relative attention bias, batch-shared negatives, positive-aware hard negatives, and boosting-based recall optimization.
    • Details: In the production A/B it gained Shop orders +0.94% relative, high-quality CTR +5% relative, conversion rate +0.71% relative, and final product recall@4 +4.8% relative verified.
    • Details: CUDA kernel optimization made the training pipeline 7.3x faster.
  • 2026-10-14 additional evidence (canon update, not radar): Meta GEM 2026 generation (blog, 2026-10-13) writes that it observed an LLM-like scaling law at its largest pretraining scale verified. A claim of 2× the performance of the previous version at the same compute is attached, so the question of this thread is shifting from "does scaling work" to "which architecture bends the curve better" inferred. → sequence-transformer-ranking.md, detailed analysis: daily/2026-10-14.md
    • Details: The sole basis of the inference above is that Meta presented the architecture comparison at the same compute.
    • Details: It swapped the backbone from InterFormer to Kunlun (arXiv 2602.10016) verified. The blog writes: "We observed an LLM-like scaling law at our largest pre-training scale with 2.5x model complexity growth from earlier versions of GEM." verified
  • 2026-10-17 additional evidence: LinkedIn TransX (arXiv 2610.20913, 2026-10-14) reports that scaling returns differ by component (anisotropic). It makes a claim in the same direction as HELIX's (a ranking architecture that grows feature interaction and long behavior sequences together) "both axes must grow together", but with a different decomposition (encoder, memory, fusion).
    • Details: If memory resolution and fusion capacity are fixed, scaling encoder depth can saturate at moderate depth verified.
    • Details: As supervision increases, the optimal allocation shifts toward candidate-conditioned fusion verified.
    • Details: The required memory resolution stays roughly constant even as the model grows verified.
    • Details: See the TransX entry under "New this month" below.

Generative retrieval / semantic ID

  • What: A family that represents items as sequences of semantic tokens rather than ID embeddings and replaces search with autoregressive generation. We confirmed only that Spotify gave a semantic tokenization talk at PRS 2025 verified.
    • Details: This talk is commonly misattributed to Netflix. See the open question in netflix.md.
  • Why notable: If this approach holds, it replaces the entire embedding store and ANN (approximate nearest neighbor search) stack. Netflix GenPage (page generation) and GenRec (verbalization) are adjacent trends verified.
  • Watch for: We await production deployment statements from follow-up work on Google/YouTube-family TIGER inferred. The original TIGER paper (RecSys 2023) has not yet been confirmed as a primary source in this repository, and we plan to check it at the next collection.
  • 2026-08-11 additional evidence: HD-Rec (arXiv 2608.06997, 2026-08-07) is research on extending SID cross-domain, with Kuaishou's senior line (Ruiming Tang, Guorui Zhou, Han Li) participating. It has only public benchmark results and no production statement yet, so we record it only as a signal that the Kuaishou line has joined the SID family.
    • Details: To extend SID cross-domain it uses a hierarchical codebook (global coarse and domain-adaptive fine) together with a domain sparse MoE.
  • 2026-08-12 additional evidence: Kuaishou PushDualGen (arXiv 2608.07989, 2026-08-08) is the next generation in the line from OneRec (Kuaishou's generative recommendation model) to OneRec-Thinking. It was deployed to production in Kuaishou push recommendation, and in the online A/B test the effective play rate rose +8.50% relative, the dissatisfaction rate fell −37.70% relative, and long-tail impressions also improved verified.
    • Details: To avoid the serving cost of CoT, it uses a 2-stage generation structure that first emits the SID and then attaches skippable explanation text (copy).
    • Details: Following HD-Rec, the Kuaishou SID line passed through the radar two days in a row. It is a signal that the SID line keeps evolving at the company level. It is also the second company, besides Netflix GenRec, to accumulate large-scale A/B statements.
  • 2026-09-01 additional evidence: Alibaba PailitaoGR (arXiv 2608.26658, 2026-08-27 v1) is an attempt to extend SID generative retrieval from product search to image search, and is the first statement of the image-search modality in the SID line. It stated an average +13.8% improvement over baseline offline but did not disclose production A/B figures verified.
    • Details: All 9 authors are affiliated with Alibaba Group Hangzhou (we confirmed affiliations in the HTML version). Pailitao is Taobao's image search surface.
    • Details: Real photographs contain the search target, supporting evidence (e.g., text inside the photo), and irrelevant visual elements together. The paper addresses this problem along two axes.
    • Details: The target enhancer emphasizes only the search target tokens on the full image without cropping, implementing "Zooming without Cropping". The auxiliary enhancer selectively extracts relevant text evidence inside the image without OCR, implementing "Reading without OCR".
    • Details: Training combines on-policy distillation and a contrastive strategy. It states that the training and validation sets were sampled from real Pailitao image search logs.
    • Details: After Kuaishou HD-Rec/PushDualGen (short video, push) and Yandex Music Gryphon-v2/Sona (music), this is the fourth company on the SID line. With no production metrics, the strength it adds to the thread is weak.
  • 2026-09-03 additional evidence: Tencent PCG TGR (arXiv 2609.00986, 2026-09-01 v1) is a 4-in-1 generative recommendation framework combining ranking, retrieval, slate generation, and reason tokens. It is a production claim counterpart to the Kuaishou OneRec family, the case with the second-largest company scale, and the first source to name HSTU and OneRec each as replacement targets within one frame. Detailed analysis: daily/2026-09-03.md
    • Details: A team of 13 TGR Team authors from Tencent Platform and Content Group (PCG) states A/B tests and production deployment on five Tencent PCG surfaces. The five surfaces are news feeds, short videos, long videos, music, and web novels, and the paper labels them as anonymous Scenario 1–5.
    • Details: CCFormer (Transformer ranking, replacing HSTU/DLRM) was fully launched in Scenario 1 and 2 verified. It gained CTR +3.57% and ad revenue up 1.71%, with half the GFLOPs of HSTU and training 2.21x faster verified.
    • Details: BARGE (hierarchical semantic ID generative retrieval) beat OneRec by 10.2–16.9% on offline Hit@5 verified. It was fully deployed as a generative retrieval channel (fully rolled out as a generative retrieval channel), with CTR +0.60% and reading time up 1.70% verified.
    • Details: HiGR (whole-slate generation) has slate quality +15.9–21.3% over OneRec and 5x inference speed. In the three-scenario A/B it gained watch time +1.22% and video views up 1.73%, but it does not yet state a production launch.
    • Details: In TGR-Reason, a Think model trained with LatentRec generates semantic-ID reason tokens offline and injects them as prompts at serving time. Cold-start new-user Hit@1 is +477.8%, and online effective consumption rose +1.75% and new-user conversion rose 13.09%.
    • Details: Items needing a deep dive are BARGE's OSQ-VAE codebook structure, the substantive difference between HiGR's PCRQ-VAE and OneRec SID, the Think model backbone and reason token corpus scale, and the Scenario→product mapping.
  • 2026-09-07 additional evidence: Spotify GLIDE is a candidate generator in which a 1B LLM generates Semantic ID token sequences and maps them to catalog items, and SPDD is a controller that intervenes only at decoding time. If Kuaishou PushDualGen avoided serving cost with SID and skippable copy, SPDD is an alternative angle that leaves the serving model as is and changes only the decoding policy. Detailed analysis: daily/2026-09-07.md
    • Details: GLIDE (From Models to Products, 2026-08-03 blog) was deployed to production on the Spotify Home podcast surface verified. In a 21-day A/B at ~20M impression scale it gained non-habitual listening +5.4% and new-show discovery +14.3% verified.
    • Details: SPDD stands for Stochastic Primal-Dual Decoding (Balancing Multiple Objectives in Generative Recommendations with Adaptive Decoding, 2026-08-07 blog). It is a lightweight controller that intervenes only at the decoding (inference) time of a pretrained generative recommender.
    • Details: SPDD dynamically adjusts the weights of auxiliary objectives (e.g., relevance and diversity, relevance and novelty) as it progresses, and does not require retraining.
    • Details: In a production A/B on Spotify SASRec playlist ranking (~1 million users × 600,000 playlists), auxiliary stream-share was +5.44%, higher than fixed-weight's +3.60%, and user consumption did not decline verified.
  • 2026-09-09 additional evidence (late discovery): Shopify Foundational Generative Recommender (Shopify Engineering, 2026-02-25) deployed an HSTU-based generative recommender to production with a large embedding table approach and no SID yet, and then stated semantic ID explicitly as the next step verified. Detailed analysis: daily/2026-09-09.md
    • Details: The reason given for naming it the next step is "products are modeled independently…very large embedding tables…bottleneck" verified.
    • Details: Kuaishou PushDualGen, Spotify GLIDE, and Tencent BARGE are follow-up improvements made after SID was already deployed. HD-Rec and PailitaoGR have no production deployment statement. Shopify, by contrast, made a statement from the stage just before SID adoption, which fits the observation that adoption proceeds sequentially among companies that feel the embedding table scale problem in practice.
  • 2026-10-15 additional evidence: WeChat WeGR (arXiv 2610.16979, 2026-10-12 v1) is generative retrieval for WeChat e-commerce search, and per the abstract it gained orders +2.539% in a 2-month online A/B verified (abstract). Because it is a search surface it is distant from feeds, but following Tlow (a flow-based item tokenizer) in "Earlier additions (2026-08–09)" below, it is the same company's second SID production statement.
    • Details: We did not read the main text. In the same A/B, CTR was +1.508%, conversion rate +1.381%, and CPU and GPU usage −25.5% and −26.1%, and it was deployed on several WeChat search surfaces verified (abstract).
    • Details: It combines an SID tokenizer (CoCo-VAE) that injects collaborative signals and prevents codebook collapse with RL (DHPO) that solves the sparsity of exact-match SID rewards by widening the reward range per SID layer. This source adds the RL design axis of "partial rewards per SID layer" to this thread.
  • 2026-10-16 counter-evidence: Shopify's Revisiting Generative vs. Dense Retrieval (arXiv 2610.19065, 2026-10-13 v1) is the first platform company source in this thread to directly rebut the superiority claim of generative retrieval (GR). It reports that most of the gap by which GR looks better than dense retrieval (DR) on Amazon Toys public data comes from popularity, and argues that mixing the same static item value once into DR scores reverses the gap verified.
    • Details: 3 authors are affiliated with Shopify (1 also holds an appointment at University of Toronto, which we confirmed in the HTML version). There is no online A/B; it is an offline comparison.
    • Details: On Shopify's in-house large-scale e-commerce data too, the comparison result is written as follows: "a 14B SID-LLM still underperforms a 47× smaller dense encoder" verified
    • Details: Gen4+N is the number these notes attach in sequence to X commits after the Gen4 release. X also runs SID retrieval alongside other channels at Gen4+34, so it is evidence for coexistence rather than replacement inferred. The basis is that in X code the SID channel was added as one source without replacing the existing two-tower (a retrieval model that embeds users and items separately).
  • 2026-10-21 additional evidence: Alibaba Taobao and Tmall's Don't Rewrite the SID Map (arXiv 2610.26105, 2026-10-19 v1) is the first operational statement to quantify that reassigning SID breaks the decoding path of an already deployed generative retrieval. A warm-start update that freezes existing item SIDs and inserts only new items gave GMV +1.16% in a 3-week A/B verified. Detailed analysis: daily/2026-10-21.md
    • Details: All 7 authors are affiliated with Taobao & Tmall Group of Alibaba (confirmed in the HTML version). The surface is e-commerce search.
    • Details: It reports that in a 67-day log experiment, with beam 1 the mapping update dropped HitRate by 41.78–62.05%, while with beam 1000 it dropped only 8.33–10.85%, so a large beam masks the degradation verified (abstract).
    • Details: In the same A/B, UCTCVR was +0.29% verified (main text). The improvements of +9% for existing items and +109% for new items are offline figures.
  • 2026-10-21 additional evidence (canon update, not radar): Meta GenRecs Tokenization (blog, 2026-10-19) replaced item representations in ads ranking and retrieval with token sequences of a "stable semantic vocabulary", and writes that together with GEM it gave conversion +0.35% verified. Together with the Alibaba paper the same day, SID vocabulary stability has risen to an operational issue inferred. → llm-in-recsys.md, detailed analysis: daily/2026-10-21.md
    • Details: The basis of the inference above is that each of the two sources puts "stable vocabulary" and "topology preservation" forward as a core design principle. However, Meta's "stable" is said in contrast to item IDs that disappear quickly, and it does not directly address the SID reassignment problem Alibaba covered.
  • 2026-10-22 additional evidence: Youku SPAR (arXiv 2610.27898, 2026-10-20 v1) re-fetches long-term history matching the prefix at each step of SID decoding, and balances the interaction load across sibling branches to reduce skew toward popular paths. In a 7-day A/B on 10% of Youku production traffic, it recorded average watch time per user +3.95% and play conversion rate +3.38% verified (abstract). Details: daily/2026-10-22.md
    • Details: It writes that the traffic-weighted share of the largest child branch was reduced by 32.84% across SID stages verified (abstract). I did not read the main text.
  • 2026-10-31 additional evidence: Kuaishou GR4AD-X (arXiv 2610.38378, 2026-10-28 v1) is a follow-up to GR4AD, the generative ads recommendation system already deployed. It reported cumulative ad revenue +3.07% over the deployed baseline in a large-scale A/B test verified (abstract). Details: daily/2026-10-31.md
    • Details: It changed three things. It assigns multiple SID paths to one item, expands capacity along several axes instead of widening or deepening the backbone, and aligns beam pruning and re-ranking with commercial value through prefix-level rewards verified (abstract). I did not read the main text.

Learned index (index-as-model) — does it move to organic feeds?

  • What: A trend of making the index a model tensor and a training target rather than a static data structure. Meta Andromeda/HILL is ads literature, and the blog post describes SilverTorch as a UGC feed and video retrieval system verified. → embedding-retrieval.md
  • Why notable: There are many precedents of stacks verified in ads moving to organic feeds. There is no first-party statement that a learned index (Andromeda/HILL) was applied to an organic feed inferred (this means no statement exists; the corpus examined refers to topics).
  • Watch for: Whether a statement appears that the same stack was applied to the organic feed, and whether a company other than Meta adopts a learned index.

New this month

ECHO (Kuaishou) — ranking that shrinks 100,000-length user history with a distillation selector

  • What: A framework that chains a distillation selector, which keeps only part of the raw events for each user, with a retriever that re-selects the relevant records for each candidate, so that a 100,000-length history can be used in target-aware ranking.
  • Why notable: In a 7-day A/B (5% of traffic) on Kuaishou's largest short-video surface (more than 400 million DAU), it recorded total app time +0.613% over the existing production system verified. This is an increment on top of a system that already uses TWIN-family long-sequence retrieval.

DrEM (Kuaishou short video) — Ensemble ranking with dual correction of pxtr noise

  • What: In the ensemble ranking stage, it corrects noise in the per-action predicted probabilities (pxtr) emitted by upstream multi-task models on both the supervision side and the feature side.

    • Prediction noise causes two problems. It can flip proxy preference labels, and it can flow in as feature-side input and shake the ranking score.
    • On the supervision side, it uses a robust loss that corrects the empirical risk with the pairwise flip probability.
    • On the feature side, it uses a preference-preserving ranking consistency regularizer over perturbations sampled from the prediction noise distribution.
    • Theoretically, it proved that the robust loss is superior to the basic pairwise loss even when the flip probability estimate has error verified.

    • The authors themselves observed that sparse behaviors such as follow and comment gain more than dense behaviors such as view and long view.
    • This observation matches the theoretical prediction. The prediction is that sparse tasks have large prediction variance and thus a high flip probability, so there is more room for robust correction.
  • Evidence: verified arXiv 2608.12778, 2026-08-13, 8 authors (a collaboration between Kuaishou Technology Beijing and Shenzhen University).

  • What: In the item tokenizer of the Semantic ID (SID) line, it approaches the three known problems of the standard method RQ-VAE with a normalizing flow. It claims to beat RQ-VAE on two axes of tokenization, codebook utilization and semantic locality verified.
    • The item tokenizer is a core component of the SID line that converts each item's semantic embedding into a discrete token ID sequence. RQ-VAE is Residual-Quantized VAE.
    • The three problems are codebook collapse, residual accumulation of quantization error, and discontinuity where similar items map to completely different token sequences.
    • Codebook collapse is the phenomenon where most code slots die and only a few are used.
    • Flow transforms the embedding space into a learnable distribution with an invertible neural network and inverts it back verified.
  • Why notable: The abstract explicitly states that a production A/B test was run on WeChat (Tencent), China's largest social media platform. The token-ID-based retrieval model recorded user CTR +10.32% globally and +11.64% for new items.
    • The larger gain on new items suggests it is especially strong at cold start. In effect, it confirms the core benefit of the semantic tokenization line in production metrics.
    • So far the SID line has mostly been a combination of "RQ-VAE + downstream model". This is the first claim that replacing the tokenizer layer with a flow-based one also improves production metrics.
    • It is also circumstantial evidence that RQ-VAE's codebook collapse was a problem in real serving inferred. We interpret this as Tencent having hit this problem and offering an alternative.
  • Evidence: verified arXiv 2608.24176, 2026-08-25 v1. The author affiliation is the Tencent WeChat rec group.
    • We confirmed the affiliation from the author list, including Chonggang Song and Nian Li, and the WeChat production claim.
  • Status: Watching. A new axis has appeared in the SID/generative retrieval thread. The tokenizer architecture itself has begun to diverge in production.
    • There are two points to watch. One is the follow-up release of code and the detailed flow structure, and the other is whether a similar tokenizer-alternative claim appears from a second company (Kuaishou/ByteDance/Xiaohongshu).
    • Tencent WeChat rec has been quiet recently. It is worth watching whether claims from other surfaces such as Weixin Video Channels follow this paper.

HEGM (VK Russia) — Short-video watch-time distribution prediction, detailed production A/B figures released

  • What: It proposes HEGM (Hierarchical Exponential-Gaussian Mixture), which predicts the conditional distribution of watch time, the core regression target of short-video ranking. It repeatedly reproduced the three failure modes of the prior SOTA EGMN and put countermeasures in place verified.
    • The conditional distribution of watch time is near-zero-inflated, long-tailed, and multimodal.
    • EGMN (Exponential-Gaussian Mixture Network) models the whole density with a Gaussian mixture. So it supports prediction of CDF threshold events rather than a single point estimate.
    • The first failure mode, variance collapse, is when σ_k(x)→0 creates a likelihood spike on particular samples.
    • The second failure mode, component redundancy, is when several Gaussians converge to μ_i≈μ_j·σ_i≈σ_j.
    • The third failure mode, inactive components, is when most mixture weights collapse to 0.
    • In HEGM, a sigmoid gate predicts the skip probability p_skip(x) = σ(W_skip h + b_skip). If skipped, the density is combined with the exponential component; otherwise, with the Gaussian mixture.
    • This hierarchical skip-watch decomposition separates "quick skip = indifference" from "engaged viewing = actual watching" at the verdict layer.
    • In addition, it adds KL-based variance regularization, applies structured initialization, and removes the forced Gaussian shift and the entropy regularizer verified.
  • Why notable: It ran a production A/B test on the large-scale short-video surface of VK (a large Russian social platform). Session depth was +9.26%, shares +14.59%, and dislikes −8.34%.
    • Results by watch length are deep watch (≥10s) +5.75%, non-short watch (≥3s) +4.59%, and skips (<3s) −6.23%.
    • In a 1.5-month test (1 month forward and 2 weeks reverse swap), 5% of traffic was allocated to each of the treatment and control groups, and each group processed ~11M requests per day verified HTML version.
    • After Kuaishou DrEM, this is a claim from another company (VK) in a short-video production ranking paper. In particular, it confirms that watch-time conditional distribution prediction (the EGMN line) is evolving as a separate lineage.
    • All authors hold joint positions at AI VK and Lomonosov Moscow State University. Russian research sources, a different company from the Yandex Music Gryphon-v2/Sona line, are accumulating production claims in succession.
  • Evidence: verified arXiv 2608.23356, submitted 2026-08-24. Code and models are released at github.com/rw404/HEGM.
  • Status: Watching. If an EGMN-family production paper appears from a second company besides VK, it becomes a candidate for introducing a section in topics/multi-task-ranking.md.
    • It is a source to compare with the Netflix Foundation Model's watch-time head and the 5-quantile head of X's ranking model Phoenix (Gen4+4).
    • Because HEGM treats the regression itself as distribution prediction, it is a different angle from Netflix and X, which combine a point estimate and quantiles.

User Behavioral Densing Law (Ant/Alipay) — scaling law confirmed by a third company

  • What: Derives a densing law between the minimum sufficient capacity of tokenization and data scale, through theory and experiments, and on that basis proposes ALGN, a variable-length approach verified.
    • It addresses the problem that when user behavior sequence data grows to billion scale, scaling raw text alone runs into diminishing returns and tokenization becomes necessary.
    • The relation between the minimum sufficient capacity C* and data scale s is ln C*(s) = β + α ln(s/s_0) in a single dimension and ln C*(s) = β + Σ_i α_i ln(s_i / s_{i,0}) in multiple dimensions.
    • ALGN adaptively adjusts tokenization capacity according to data scale verified.
  • Why notable: Following the scaling confirmations from the Meta HSTU·Wukong·GEM family and the Netflix Foundation Model, it formalized a scaling law using Ant Group (Alipay) data. However, it has only offline benchmarks, no production A/B claim, and it did not disclose the deployment status.
    • The new angle this time is a law for tokenization capacity scale rather than parameter scale.
    • It carries the naming and formula structure of the Densing Law in the LLM domain (arXiv 2412.04315) over unchanged to tokenization in the recommendation domain.
    • The offline benchmark consists of a 0.5M user held-out set and 3 downstream tasks (classification, text retrieval, user-to-user retrieval).
  • Evidence: verified arXiv 2608.23392, submitted 2026-08-24. A collaboration between the DeepFind Team @ Ant Group and Zhejiang University.
  • Status: Watching. It adds a third angle, tokenization capacity scaling, to the scaling law thread. If another Ant Group production claim appears (for example, a follow-up saying this densing law was folded into the Alipay serving stack), it becomes a promotion candidate for topics/sequence-transformer-ranking.md.

Descriptive reasoning traces reduce recommendation effectiveness (Spotify Research) — first rebuttal claim for the SID+CoT line

  • What: Uses a controlled experiment to check whether the recent trend of attaching chain-of-thought reasoning traces to Semantic ID (SID)-based generative recommenders (SIDReasoner and others) actually improves traditional offline metrics. Introducing explicit reasoning traces decreases traditional offline effectiveness in both SFT and RL verified.
    • The design is a 2×2 factorial: {SID vs Title item representation} × {No Reasoning vs Reasoning}, compared on a common Qwen3-1.7B backbone across 3 Amazon domains.
    • For example, for Office Products with Titles, R@10 dropped from .1587 to .1416 (−11%). Across all domains, Titles fell by −19% to 0% and SIDs by −9% to 0%.
    • Title produces more grounded and interpretable traces than SID. Even so, extensive SID alignment only raises trace quality and does not restore recommendation effectiveness.
    • A richer reward signal partially narrows this gap verified.
  • Why notable: Kuaishou PushDualGen claimed improved production metrics by combining SID with skippable copy (CoT-lite). In contrast, the Spotify Research controlled experiment reports the opposite observation: CoT lowers recommendation effectiveness.
    • The metrics PushDualGen claimed are effective play rate +8.50% and dissatisfaction rate −37.70%.
    • However, the two sources differ in conditions. Kuaishou ran a production A/B on the push surface with a real catalog, while Spotify used Amazon reviews and a small (1.7B) backbone offline. The goal is to work out under which conditions the negative result holds.
    • Spotify produced a reproducible rebuttal point: "attaching traces lowers the metrics, and adding more alignment does not help." So when reading the next production claim on the SID+CoT line, the conditions of backbone size, the offline-versus-online gap, and reward design are what matter.
  • Evidence: verified arXiv 2608.23154, submitted 2026-08-24. All 6 authors are affiliated with Spotify Research.
    • We confirmed the affiliation in the HTML version from the email domain @spotify.com.
  • Status: Watching. llm-in-recsys.md links it as a counterpoint to the branch that combines SID and reasoning. If a similar rebuttal or conditional confirmation claim appears from a second company, it becomes a promotion candidate for a separate section in the topic (SID and reasoning traces: production vs benchmark debate).

GateDiffInt (Xiaohongshu) — a CVR ranker applying diffusion denoising to user behavior sequences plus LLM multi-intent distillation

  • What: Addresses the noise-intent coupling problem in user behavior sequences with two components, diffusion-based denoising and LLM multi-intent distillation, and predicts CVR.
    • Noise-intent coupling is the problem that noise dilutes the true intent, and without a structured intent prior there is no denoising target either.
    • Gate-Mediated Controllable Diffusion enhances and denoises the behavior sequence through a controllable forward diffusion process with dual gating.
    • Multi-Intent LLM Distillation has an LLM teacher distill four kinds of structured intents into a lightweight student. The four kinds are long-term / short-term / latent / conversion.
    • The distilled intents are fused by attention to predict CVR verified.
  • Why notable: It is deployed on Xiaohongshu's production primary traffic, serves hundreds of millions of DAU, and claims "substantial GMV improvements". The abstract does not disclose concrete uplift numbers.
    • Where OneModel is the angle of unifying rankers across organic, ads, and shops, this one is independent, at the angle of the same company's behavior sequence encoder and CVR head.
    • It uses the LLM only as a teacher rather than placing it in serving, so it is a cousin of MetaStrategy, an offline policy compiler.
  • Evidence: verified arXiv 2608.18764, 2026-08-19 v1 / 2026-08-20 v2. The author email domain is xiaohongshu.com (confirmed from the PDF metadata).
  • Status: Watching. If a second company publishes a production ranker claim that uses diffusion for sequence denoising, it becomes a promotion candidate for a new section in topics/sequence-transformer-ranking.md. We plan to check whether concrete uplift numbers are released after v3.
    • Xiaohongshu issued two production claims (OneModel and GateDiffInt) in a short period. The two address different layers: ranker unification and the sequence encoder.
