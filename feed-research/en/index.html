feed-research

Teardowns of big-tech feed algorithms. Primary sources only, and every claim carries an evidence grade (verified observed inferred). A batch job reviews newly published material every night at 01:00 KST. These pages are machine translations (Claude) of the Korean originals, checked automatically for links, numbers, evidence grades and code. As of 2026-11-09.
